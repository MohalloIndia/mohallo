# Mohallo UI/UX Redesign Brief

## Design objective

Make Mohallo look and behave like a real product for local businesses rather than an early-stage promotional site.

## Brand constraint

The current loading logo is locked.

Everything around it can be improved.

## Product personality

- modern
- grounded
- local
- confident
- simple
- trustworthy

## Avoid

- generic startup landing-page clichés
- excessive gradients
- excessive floating blobs
- decorative AI motifs
- too many emojis
- over-rounded everything
- dozens of equally important cards

## Homepage structure

### Section 1 — Hero

Headline:
**Your local business deserves a digital shop.**

Supporting line:
Explain that Mohallo gives a small business a simple place online and helps local customers discover it.

Actions:
**Create your shop**
**Explore local shops**

### Section 2 — Product preview

Show an actual-looking digital shop interface:
- shop name
- category
- local context
- products
- contact/order CTA

This should be a product visual, not an abstract blob.

### Section 3 — Merchant journey

3–4 steps:
1. Create your shop
2. Add products/services
3. Get discovered
4. Receive enquiries/orders

### Section 4 — Why Mohallo

Focus on:
- low setup effort
- local discovery
- simple storefront
- future automation

### Section 5 — Plans

₹199/month
₹399/month

Make differences obvious.

### Section 6 — Final CTA

Strong merchant CTA with minimal copy.

## Marketplace

The customer side is intentionally secondary for this phase, but it should still be visually coherent.

Do not invent traction, ratings, review counts, or inventory.

## Seller dashboard

Make it feel like a lightweight business operating system:
- status
- tasks
- products
- demand/orders
- plan
- future Autopilot entry

## Seller onboarding

This is the primary redesign interaction.

The flow should feel like a guided setup, not a registration wall.
