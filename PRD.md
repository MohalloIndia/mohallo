# Mohallo Redesign PRD

## Goal

Improve the existing Mohallo product so that a small local business can understand, join, configure, pay for, and manage its digital shop with low friction.

## Scope

### In scope
- public website redesign
- responsive redesign
- seller onboarding
- subscription plan UI
- payment selection UX
- seller dashboard redesign
- existing marketplace presentation
- states and accessibility

### Explicitly deferred
- new buyer/customer product strategy
- advanced buyer accounts
- new delivery network
- broad social/community features
- unfinalized AI feature expansion

## Subscription

Current required pricing:
- ₹199/month
- ₹399/month

The benefits should remain configuration-driven until final entitlements are approved.

## Merchant onboarding

Three steps:

### Step 1
Personal Information

### Step 2
Shop Information

### Step 3
Payment & Integration

Completion should lead to a clear success state and seller dashboard.

## Success metrics

Early UX metrics:
- onboarding completion
- plan-selection completion
- payment-start to payment-success ratio
- merchant shop activation
- time to complete onboarding
- form error rate
